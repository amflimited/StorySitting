The service

StorySitting arranges consent-first family oral-history projects. Services may include permission handling, scheduling, disclosed AI phone interviewing, recording, transcription, editing, human quality review, Story Drops, source maps, digital chapters, family collaboration, exports, and optional printed products.

Schedules and deliverable length are estimates. Speech clarity, call length, family corrections, sensitive content, provider outages, and storyteller availability can affect completion.

Permission belongs to the storyteller

A sponsor confirms that they have a genuine relationship or legitimate reason to request contact with the adult they identify. That request is not consent from the storyteller.

The storyteller independently chooses whether StorySitting may contact them, use an AI interviewer, record, transcribe, edit, or share each story with named family members. We may reconfirm permission on every call. A “no,” uncertainty, confusion, stop request, or do-not-call request ends the relevant activity.

The sponsor may not impersonate the storyteller, bypass a refusal, provide a number for harassment, or use StorySitting to contact someone subject to a protective order or other no-contact restriction.

Contributors confirm that they have permission to share uploaded photographs, recordings, documents, recipes, and other files for this family project. Do not submit material you have no right to use.

After payment, the family may use the delivered files for private family purposes. Public, commercial, defamatory, deceptive, or impersonating use is not authorized by these terms.

Memory, accuracy, and editing

Oral history records a person’s recollection. It is not a verified genealogy, legal record, medical assessment, or promise that every memory is objectively complete. Family members may remember the same event differently.

StorySitting may remove repetition, reorder material for readability, and make light grammatical edits while preserving meaning and voice. We do not promise perfect transcription. Names, dates, privacy, and material factual corrections should be raised during review.

We may pause delivery for human review when a call includes sensitive allegations, apparent confusion, threats, abuse, self-harm, financial information, or other content that should not be automatically published.

Safe and acceptable use

  • Do not use StorySitting for surveillance, deception, coercion, impersonation, financial solicitation, therapy, emergency response, or evidence gathering.
  • Do not ask the interviewer to collect passwords, account numbers, security answers, or secrets unrelated to the family story.
  • Do not upload malware, unlawful material, or private information you are not authorized to share.
  • Do not attempt to extract system prompts, disrupt calls, abuse providers, or evade access controls.

We may refuse, pause, or close a project when safety, consent, legality, or service integrity is uncertain.
