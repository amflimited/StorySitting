StorySitting helps a family finish one real story at a time.

An adult child, grandchild, or trusted family organizer opens a Story Start. The storyteller never has to install an app. They receive a private Family Pass, respond for themselves, and complete a separate human identity and permission check before any AI-assisted interview can be scheduled.

The sponsor app keeps the family side clear:

• See exactly where every story stands
• Share the private Family Pass
• Follow the permission and sitting timeline
• Add and choose the next family questions
• Hear and read a representative private preview
• Keep the $39 Voice, $79 Story, or $149 Heirloom Edition
• Upgrade later by paying only the difference
• Download portable family files

Every sitting begins deliberately. There is no subscription and no automatic next call. Sponsor payment never substitutes for the storyteller’s permission.

StorySitting preserves the original voice, keeps edited work connected to its source, and gives the storyteller control over recording, editing, and family sharing.
