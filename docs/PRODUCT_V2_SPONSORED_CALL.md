# StorySitting v2 — Sponsored story calls

## Product sentence

They answer the phone. We turn the conversation into a story their family can keep.

StorySitting is a managed, consent-first oral-history service. An adult child or grandchild sponsors the work. The storyteller keeps control of the call, recording, edits, and sharing. StorySitting owns the work required to turn a conversation into a finished, source-faithful chapter.

## The three people in every project

### Sponsor

The daughter, son, grandchild, or other trusted adult who starts the project, provides context and questions, pays, follows progress, and receives the finished story after the storyteller approves sharing.

### Storyteller

The parent, grandparent, or loved one whose voice and memories are being recorded. The storyteller does not need an app. They independently authorize contact, AI interviewing, recording, transcription, editing, and family sharing. They can pause, skip, revoke permission, or keep a story private.

### StorySitting

The service that handles permission, scheduling, patient phone interviewing, transcription, source-faithful editing, human quality review, delivery, corrections, and the next story.

## Locked offer

- **$5 Story Start:** begins the trusted permission process and covers the first outreach/connection work. It is never represented as the storyteller's consent.
- **$79 Finished Sitting unlock:** after the storyteller authorizes, StorySitting conducts one interview and prepares a private result preview. The sponsor pays $79 only if they choose to unlock and keep the full original recording, source-linked transcript, polished digital chapters, Story Drop, and one factual correction pass.
- **$79 Next Finished Sitting:** another deliberately requested story result, unlocked only by a new purchase. No recurring subscription and no automatic next call.
- **Optional print:** priced separately after enough approved chapters exist.

Authorization does not trigger the $79 charge. StorySitting conducts the authorized sitting and prepares a private preview first. The sponsor then chooses whether to pay $79 to unlock and keep the full result. If the family unlocks it but still does not want the finished result after the correction pass, the $79 is refunded. The $5 pays for work already performed and is not refundable after outreach begins.

## Safe permission handshake

A sponsor can request contact but cannot consent for the storyteller. Before any automated or AI-voiced outbound interview, StorySitting must have prior consent from the called person. The domain model supports three permission paths:

1. **Family Pass:** the sponsor sends a personal introduction and four-digit family code from their own phone. The storyteller responds online, then a direct human check independently verifies identity and permission before scheduling.
2. **Human hello:** a live Story Sitter makes the short first contact, explains the gift, and records the storyteller's contact preference. The AI interviewer only calls later, after authorization.
3. **Call us:** the storyteller initiates the call to the verified StorySitting line and authorizes the next scheduled interview.

The v0.3 launch interface offers only Family Pass followed by a direct human identity check. The other paths stay unavailable until their delivery, caller-matching, and operator workflows are complete.

The interview opens with a fresh AI disclosure and recording confirmation every time. A refusal, confusion, or request to stop immediately ends that Story Start. A separate, explicit choice adds the number to StorySitting's permanent do-not-call list.

## Customer lifecycle

1. Sponsor chooses the storyteller and pays $5.
2. Sponsor adds a personal introduction, safe family code, preferred times, and three to five story seeds.
3. Storyteller completes the permission handshake.
4. The app shows permission received and the interview time.
5. StorySitting conducts a roughly 30-minute sitting and prepares the result without an automatic $79 charge.
6. A private preview arrives: the strongest passage and a clear view of the finished result.
7. The sponsor deliberately pays $79 if they want to unlock and keep the full Story Drop, recording, transcript, source-linked chapters, and one correction pass.
8. Storyteller controls sharing; sponsor can request factual corrections after unlock.
9. Family listens, reads, adds a photograph, or queues one next question.
10. A new sitting happens only when the family deliberately requests another; no call or charge repeats automatically.

## The app's job

The storyteller should never need the app. The app is the family's production studio and living Story Shelf.

- See the exact permission and call status without pestering the storyteller.
- Record a personal introduction for the permission handshake.
- Add and vote on questions for the next call.
- Receive a Story Drop after every sitting.
- Switch among edited chapter, verbatim transcript, and source audio.
- Trace important lines to the moment they were spoken.
- Mark names, dates, places, and uncertain details for review.
- Invite relatives with story-level access rather than exposing the entire archive.
- Export the recording, transcript, approved chapter, photographs, and permission record.
- Ask the next question and buy another sitting without starting over.

## Differentiation

Phone access, AI follow-up questions, transcription, books, and family collaboration are now category basics. StorySitting must own four promises:

1. **Permission you can see.** The storyteller, not the payer, controls participation and sharing.
2. **Every polished line has a source.** Original audio, verbatim transcript, and edited prose stay connected.
3. **We finish the work.** This is not 52 weeks of family homework or an archive the customer must organize.
4. **One call creates the next good question.** People, places, eras, objects, and open threads become a living Story Thread map.

The numbers below are operating assumptions to validate with real orders, not accounting claims.

| Finished Sitting unlock | $79 | $6 or less incremental | full processing, storage, payment fee, delivery |
| Human QA after unlock | included | 10 minutes or less | names, dates, unsupported prose, delivery check |

At an illustrative Retell configuration of about $0.11 per connected minute, a six-minute permission interaction plus a 30-minute interview is about $4 in voice infrastructure before transcription, preview generation, and payment fees. StorySitting therefore carries some result-production cost before knowing whether the sponsor will unlock it. Track preview-to-$79-unlock conversion, cost per authorized preview, full-processing cost, and human time from day one. The unlock rate—not just call completion—determines whether this model works.

## Non-negotiable trust rules

- Never ask the storyteller for money, passwords, account numbers, or security answers.
- Never clone or synthesize the storyteller's voice.
- Never publish or train on family material without a separate, explicit opt-in.
- Preserve original audio; edited prose is never the sole record.
- Treat sponsor authorization, contact authorization, recording consent, AI-processing consent, and family-sharing permission as separate events.
- Make export, deletion, revocation, and do-not-call controls available.
- Stop when a storyteller sounds confused or distressed and route the project to human review.
- Show uncertainty and parallel memories; do not force conflicting recollections into one false account.
